# A published first read

Hyperdrift ran the method on its own site before offering it to anyone else:
https://orchestra.hyperdrift.io/traction/first-read

Read it for the shape, not the content. Notice:

- The strength names something a stranger can check: a live tool call on the
  homepage with its raw inputs and result one click away.
- The constraint carries measurements: the first call to action at 1,450 px on
  a 390 px phone, the form heading at 4,644 px on an 844 px screen.
- The next customer move is a move with people, with a success count (3 of the
  first 10 founders ask for a next step) and a way to read a miss (fewer than 5
  of 10 send their link: reword the offer first).
- The read says what it could not see: analytics are private, the search check
  used a public index.
- A later note records what changed after the read, dated, and separates the
  changes from their effect, which had not been measured.
